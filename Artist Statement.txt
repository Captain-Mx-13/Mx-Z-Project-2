The goal with this project very quickly shifted into finding a way for me to almost grapple or control the idea of randomization as much as possible. The two big ways I used randomization is in choosing what story is shown and in the background images I have created. This is where I was able to find some balance in the randomization, I created the images, so had control over what was going to be shown and I made it so that one story would be chosen and then would move to completion, keeping the story and its creative intent intact.
As the project went on however I also found some ways to touch on randomization and patterns more thematically. The images I created for the background serve 2 purposes, the first is that they are simply things or symbols I enjoy including in my artwork regularly, the second is that they are things that are associated with patterns. The blooming of flowers, the placement of stars (like constellations), the repetition of waves, all of these are patterns in their own rights. 
While this project was quite challenging for me conceptually I think I ultimately succeeded in finding ways to compromise with the idea of randomness and include my own interests and creative style.
